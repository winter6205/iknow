# Spec: Permission-axis semantics — what each axis controls, and what every combination means

**Status:** ready for review (rev 2, 2026-10-15 — the axis inventory, the preset table and every posture's entry are revised for ADR-0141; `draft` when this spec was opened under ADR-0139 / ADR-0140)
**Basis:** ADR-0141 (yolo and `--no-sandbox` are presets on one session overlay — the tiers, the preset table, the invariant and the marker derivation below) / ADR-0139 (the permission axis is fence-independent) / ADR-0140 (`full_auto` is bounded) / ADR-0119 (yolo; amended) / ADR-0092 (fs isolation modes) / ADR-0032 (the product word "auto mode" is `full_auto`) / ADR-0030 (graph overlay) / ADR-0068 (hard wall) / ADR-0127 (security review) / ADR-0090 (project-layer rule)

**Why this spec exists:** the permission axis had no home. Its three values were defined by a file-header comment (`src/harness/permission/modes.ts`) and about a dozen ADRs between them, and the two facts that mattered most — that yolo drives it (ADR-0119) and that `full_auto` is unbounded (`policy.ts`) — were carried by prose that no single place owned. Three ADRs had to be read together to answer "what does `full_auto` actually do", and reading them together produced a contradiction after 2026-10-06: ADR-0119 said entering yolo writes `full_auto`, while ADR-0140 describes a `full_auto` with a boundary question that only exists when the fence is up. This spec is the single answer, and it is where the next change lands.

> **Amended 2026-10-15 by ADR-0141 (operator rulings 1–9).** That paragraph's closing claim is now literal: ADR-0141 changed the answer to **what does `--yolo` mean** again, and that question is this spec's subject. `yolo` stops being a value of either axis and becomes the name of a **preset**; the fence axis's second input is a `fenceRetired` boolean **derived from the effective configuration**; and ADR-0139 §2's no-fence-with-questions posture keeps its definition but loses its `--yolo` entry, because ADR-0141 named a flag for its content instead — `--no-sandbox`. The combinations table is where all three consequences are recorded.

---

## The two axes

The product has two independent runtime axes that both shape what a call may do. Neither writes the other (ADR-0139). What pairs them up is a **preset**, which sits above both — the next section.

**The fence axis** — `fsMode` (global / workspace) plus **`fenceRetired`**, a boolean **derived from the effective configuration** and never stored as an entry label (ADR-0141 ruling 6). It answers: _what is the process physically contained by?_ A fence is a mount-and-netns construction (`src/harness/sandbox/bwrap.ts`); `fenceRetired` retires it wholesale, for all four routes, at one factory branch (the option field `src/harness/sandbox/bwrap.ts:215`, the sole bare-argv branch `:724`). `yolo` is **not** a member of this axis — it is the name of a preset — which is what ADR-0119 ruling 1's "independent boolean state axis" always meant and now literally implements: the axis reads a fence fact, not a product label.

**The permission axis** — `PermissionMode` (`default` / `plan` / `full_auto`). It answers: _is this call put to the operator?_ It has three values and no fourth — the domain is closed at `src/harness/permission/modes.ts`.

Two things sit **beneath** both axes and are not axes:

- **Hard walls** (`src/harness/permission/hard-walls.ts`) — pre-execution, spelling-sensitive denials that no mode, grant, or fence setting overrides.
- **Security review** (ADR-0127) — the ambiguity channel for what the parser cannot decide; it asks even where a mode would allow.

They intercept **as before, in every preset and outside one** (`src/harness/permission/policy.ts:413` and `:422`, both above every mode branch; ADR-0141 ruling 1). A preset is an **axis combination**, never a guardrail bypass.

## The three tiers and the presets

Every value on both axes resolves through one chain (ADR-0141 ruling 2): **persisted settings < startup flag < session overlay**. The overlay is the tier a preset writes: memory-only, never written to settings or to a session file, and with **no snapshot and no restore** — the snapshot channel that produced F4, the defect this decision closes, is deleted rather than guarded.

There are exactly **two presets** (ADR-0141 ruling 1):

| Preset         | Fence   | `fsMode` | Permission    | Entries                                           |
| -------------- | ------- | -------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **yolo**       | retired | `global` | `full_auto`   | `--yolo` (launch, no modal); `/yolo` + modal      |
| **no-sandbox** | retired | `global` | **untouched** | `--no-sandbox` (launch, no modal; no `/` command) |

- **`--auto-mode` is not a preset.** It is a permission-axis-only startup choice (`src/cli.ts:1095` maps it to `permissionMode: "full_auto"`, and nothing else); it retires no fence, and no fence fact enters it.
- **`--eval-state` is declarative application of the yolo preset at startup** (`src/harness/sandbox/eval-state.ts:216-236`): it writes the overlay directly, never goes through the interactive controller, and has **no exit** — an eval invocation's posture ends with the process (`src/harness/sandbox/eval-state.ts:210-214` records why there is nothing to probe). Its own permission write (`:234`) is absorbed into the preset instead of being a second carrier; the resulting triple is the one eval applies today, so **no ADR-0130 §2 measurement claim moves** and published numbers stay comparable.
- Both presets are reachable **only from the TUI** (`--eval-state` excepted, headless by design): a non-TUI command carrying either flag is a typed error with a non-zero exit and nothing started — the shape already implemented for `--yolo` at `src/cli/parse-args.ts:890-895`, extended to `--no-sandbox`. The confirmation modal belongs to **in-session** entry and to no launch flag: the flag typed in a shell is itself the explicit act (`src/tui/app.tsx:2162` opens the modal from `/yolo`; `applyYoloLaunchSeed` → `enterAtLaunch` at `src/tui/run.tsx:411-416` / `:722` has no modal face).
- **A preset and a flag pair are different objects.** `--no-sandbox` with `--auto-mode` arrives at the yolo preset's triple from two tiers and is allowed (ADR-0141 ruling 1): it shows **无沙箱 + `Auto` with no YOLO badge** — the `--no-sandbox` preset _is_ active and keeps its own clear, while a permission change clears nothing because no preset in the pair declares the permission axis. `plan` at the same time stays flag-unreachable — there is no `--permission-mode` flag, the wheel never lands on `plan` (`src/harness/permission/modes.ts:125-128`), and the project tier's enum is `default` / `plan` only (`src/harness/permission/project-settings.ts:48`).

**The invariant** (ruling 4): **effective `fsMode = workspace` ⇒ the fence is not retired.** It is asserted on **every effective-value computation**, and a violation is a **typed error, never a silent correction**. It is a construction property rather than a repair: both presets declare `fsMode: global` in the overlay together with the retirement, so `workspace` and a retired fence are never simultaneously effective and the assertion has nothing to correct — it fires only if a later change reintroduces a retirement path that forgets to declare the tier.

**Whole-clear, per declared axis** (ruling 3): a user change to an axis **the preset declares** **exits the preset** — the entire overlay is cleared (synchronously, before the settings write-back), the user's choice is written to settings (a write-back failure shows a notice; the effective value stays the user's choice and the overlay is not re-seeded), the effective values are recomputed, and the preset's markers are dropped. yolo declares fence, `fsMode` **and** permission, so it exits on a tier write or on a permission change; no-sandbox declares two and **survives a permission change**, because a clear resolves a _contradiction_ between declaration and choice and `--no-sandbox` never declared anything about permission — clearing there would take the fence back for a reason the posture never claimed. Partial clearing is forbidden, and the reason is the invariant: clearing the tier while the overlay still retires the fence manufactures exactly the illegal state ruling 4 exists to prevent. Two consequences the posture table depends on: a mid-session permission change during the **yolo** preset puts the fence back, so no preset can be steered into `plan` _while remaining a preset_; during the **no-sandbox** preset the same change leaves the fence retired, which is what gives posture 5 an in-session route at all. The **hook's reach is settled** (ADR-0141 ruling 3's amendment): it sits at every site that applies a user's choice to a declared axis — the `fsMode` persist sites (`src/tui/app.tsx:1117-1118`, `:1356-1360`; sibling writers `:1157` subagentCap and `:1192` worktreeOnMutate write axes no preset declares and carry no clear hook) for the tier, and for permission the two flip surfaces (`/permissions set` at `src/harness/permission/permissions-command.ts:109-111`, the Shift+Tab wheel's `permission.set` at `src/harness/graph/mode.ts:189`) plus the new write-back, so flip + write + clear are one action. A hook that sees only a future settings write would leave a keystroke during the yolo preset silently shadowed by the overlay's declaration — the mode row keeping its old label while the operator presses for another — which is F4 in a new axis. Two traps at that surface: the live wheel helper is `applyShiftTabAgentModeFlip` (`src/harness/graph/mode.ts:172-191`), while the single-axis `applyShiftTabModeFlip` (`src/harness/permission/modes.ts:168-180`) is exported and **never called** from `src/tui/` or `src/cli/`; and the wheel must be read as its **result**, since its `full_auto → full_auto + graph` step (`:155-157`) changes only the graph axis, which no preset owns, and must clear nothing; and its **read** must come from the **effective** permission value, because `applyShiftTabAgentModeFlip` takes its starting point from the holder today (`src/harness/graph/mode.ts:183`) and a press inside a preset would otherwise step from the pre-preset value, move nothing on the row, and still clear the posture (ADR-0141 §3).

The tiers, stated as what actually exists (verified at f8e56652), because a ruling about a chain that does not exist would be decoration:

- **There is no `--fs-mode` startup flag.** Persisted settings are `fsMode`'s only startup source (`src/tui/run.tsx:704` → `resolveFsIsolationMode`, `src/config/settings.ts:646-650`; the declared field is `src/config/settings.ts:495`), so `fsMode`'s middle tier is realized only by a preset applied at launch — today `enterAtLaunch` (`src/harness/sandbox/yolo.ts:350-359`), under ruling 2 an overlay seed.
- **Permission's chain is explicit > `IKNOW_PERMISSION_MODE` > project `defaultMode` > `default`** (`src/cli/runtime.ts:60-73`, called for the TUI at `src/tui/run.tsx:684`), and the explicit value comes only from `--auto-mode`. Its bottom tier is today a project file, not a user settings key.
- **`IKNOW_FS_MODE` and `IKNOW_YOLO` are not startup layers.** They are parent→worker wires written at `src/harness/subagent/spawn.ts:248` / `:253-255` and read only inside the worker (`src/harness/subagent/worker.ts:508`, `:540-546`): a host value cannot seed a session, and an ambient one is scrubbed before the write.

Two implementation obligations follow from verified gaps, not from preference (ADR-0141 ruling 3, Pending P3):

- **A permission settings write-back has to be built.** `src/config/persist-settings.ts` exports patches and writers for thinking / memory / fsMode / worktreeOnMutate / subagentCap / model only, and **both** permission channels flip the holder alone — `/permissions` (`src/tui/app.tsx:1429-1443`) and the Shift+Tab wheel (`src/harness/graph/mode.ts:189`) — so ruling 3's "write the user's choice to settings" has no permission channel to call today. When it lands it is a **user-layer** write: it puts `full_auto` on disk for the first time, and ADR-0090's prohibition must keep holding there — the project layer may not self-grant the mode (`src/harness/permission/project-settings.ts:156-164`; its declared `defaultMode` enum is `default` / `plan`, `:48`).
- **The overlay must not be routed through native session state.** `src/session-api/store/native-state-store.ts:294-319` already refuses any persisted field matching `…|yolo` (enforced by `forbiddenPersistedField`, `:319`), **and that pattern must be widened to the `no-sandbox` / `fenceRetired` spellings** (ADR-0141 §2) — otherwise a preset declaration persists under a name the guard never matched. Recorded because "no persistence" is a ruling — ADR-0119 ruling 8, which now binds the **overlay** instead of a holder — and not a trap for an implementation to discover later.

## The fence axis

| Value                                                               | Meaning                                                                                                                                | Entered by                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **global**                                                          | Host real paths readable and writable below the physically protected targets.                                                          | default; `/config fs global`                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **workspace**                                                       | Home visible but read-only; writes are the live `taskRoot` ∪ session tmp. A write outside that is refused by the mount, not by a rule. | `/config fs workspace`                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **fence retired** (`fenceRetired`, with `fsMode` declared `global`) | The fence retires entirely: bare argv, no mount layers, no netns, no egress seam. All four routes run bare.                            | the **yolo preset** — `--yolo` (launch), or `/yolo` + modal; the **no-sandbox preset** — `--no-sandbox` (launch, no in-session command yet); `--eval-state`, the yolo preset applied headlessly under its own name |

The `fsMode` value and the fence-retirement boolean are **not one setting**: a `workspace` tier has no fence to carry it, so retirement always arrives with `global` — but as **two facts declared together in the overlay**, never as one axis rewriting the other. What changed is the mechanism. Today the force is a rewrite whose provenance no later read can recover: `applyEnterCombination` sets `FS_ISOLATION_MODE_DEFAULT` (`"global"`, `src/harness/sandbox/fs-mode.ts:55`) only when the current tier differs (`src/harness/sandbox/yolo.ts:326-331`) — so "already `global`" means "no write at all" — and exit then restores a snapshot over whatever the user chose in between (`src/harness/sandbox/yolo.ts:373-375`). That is F4. Under ADR-0141 `global` is a **preset declaration in a tier the user cannot write**: the conditional write's observable consequence survives (a session already on `global` shows no tier change either way), and its unrecoverable-provenance half does not, because there is no snapshot left to re-apply.

## The permission axis

| Value           | Read-only calls | Mutating calls                                                                                | Label     |
| --------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| **`default`**   | allow           | **ask** (the operator decides per call)                                                       | Default   |
| **`plan`**      | allow           | **deny, without asking**                                                                      | Plan Mode |
| **`full_auto`** | allow           | allow **inside the current `fsMode`'s reach**; **at the edge of it, one question** (ADR-0140) | Auto      |

`plan` is deliberately not a Shift+Tab wheel station: a planning session must not be flushed by a stray keypress. It is entered and left by explicit command, from every surface that offers a mode switch.

## Does

- **Own the definition of each mode's behaviour** in one place, so that a change to `full_auto` semantics is a change here first.
- **Own the preset table.** Which axis combinations count as presets, what each one declares, and which entry reaches which posture — that is this spec's claim; `specs/yolo-mode.md` owns the yolo preset's implementation contract (its entries, modal, marker copy and argv), and ADR-0141 owns the rulings. A third preset, or a new entry for an existing posture, is a change here first.
- **Name every reachable combination.** An unnamed reachable posture is the failure mode that produced the original coupling.
- **Record the boundary question's home** — the permission decision path, where every other "does this call need the operator" question is already decided and where the existing ask route carries the answer. There is **one** boundary decision: the fence still physically enforces the mode's reach (ADR-0092 rejects duplicating a boundary across layers, not deciding it before the call runs), and this decision is an additional refusal source, not a substitute for the fence. The decision must be available **before** the call executes — an answer that arrives after the kernel has already refused cannot widen that call.
- **Distinguish the two refusals** a `[fs_denied]`-shaped message can carry:
  - a **protected-target** refusal (physical, unconditional, no route — `specs/effect-boundary-protection.md`'s contract), and
  - a **mode-boundary** refusal (this call left the current `fsMode`'s reach — **answerable**, per ADR-0140).
    They share the `[fs_denied]` prefix family and **must not share a sentence**: a permanent-sounding refusal about a temporary condition tells the agent there is no narrower spelling to try, which is the one thing it needs to know.
- **Record the two gates' deliberate differences** from the egress side: the network gate remembers a decision for the session; the file-boundary gate does not, because `fsMode` is a mid-session holder and a remembered answer would outlive its condition (ADR-0140 §3).

## Does not

- **Change the mode domain.** Three values, `full_auto` keeps its name (`src/harness/permission/modes.ts`; ADR-0119's non-goal list carries this forward). A fourth mode is tracked separately (issue #1214).
- **Change what the hard wall or the security review do.** They are below both axes and stay there.
- **Change either preset's entry enumeration, its non-persistence, or its no-half-bare rule.** ADR-0119's ruling 1 and Amendment 2026-09-20 (ii) are the only clauses ADR-0139 amended; ADR-0141's pointers, clause by clause, cited by ruling number because that ADR carries inline amendment markers and its line numbers move: rulings **2** (all four routes bare at one factory), **3** (the egress seam skipped wholesale), **4** (the worktree gate not skipped) and **5** (the mode-row badge slot, no new bottom-bar row — the slot now carries the **derived** marker) stand verbatim; **7** (TUI-only entries with typed refusals) is **extended**, so the refusal covers `--no-sandbox` as well as `--yolo`; **6**'s snapshot channel is **replaced** by the overlay's whole-clear; **8**'s no-persistence stands and now **binds the overlay**, not a holder.
- **Let a preset bypass the hard wall or the security review**, and **let one re-fence processes that are already running.** A preset is an axis combination (Does, above); both pre-filters intercept in every preset and outside one (`src/harness/permission/policy.ts:413`, `:422`). A fence change reaches only commands started after it (ADR-0141 ruling 8), at these granularities:

  | Route                  | Re-read point                                                             | Granularity                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
  | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | foreground bash (main) | `src/harness/aci/tools/bash.ts:418`, frozen per call at `:1307-1312`      | the next call; an in-flight call keeps the argv it was assembled with                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
  | background spawn       | the same snapshot, handed on at `src/harness/aci/tools/bash.ts:1535-1538` | per spawn, frozen for that task's life                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
  | subagent worker        | `src/harness/subagent/spawn.ts:254`, per spawn                            | the next spawn; a live worker keeps its own private holder                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
  | verify sandbox-run     | `src/harness/verify/sandbox-run.ts:152`                                   | **per `runVerifyLoop` entry (≈ per user turn)** — `src/harness/verify/verify-loop.ts:1556-1557` builds the closure once and every correction round reuses it, contradicting `src/harness/verify/sandbox-run.ts:149-151`'s comment that the caller rebuilds it per round (ADR-0141 Pending P2: a comment defect, not a behavior) |

- **Define the protected-target inventory** — `specs/effect-boundary-protection.md` owns it.
- **Grant the project layer the ability to set `defaultMode: "full_auto"`** — ADR-0090's fail-loud stands (`src/harness/permission/project-settings.ts:156-164`), and it is the reason ruling 3's new write-back must be user-layer.

- `src/harness/sandbox/yolo.ts` — the fence-axis entry; ADR-0139 removed its permission write. - `src/harness/sandbox/` + `src/config/` — the overlay and the effective-value computation live here, below the TUI (ADR-0141 ruling 6's layering: `src/tui/` renders what these derive and never computes the marker itself — AGENTS.md hard boundary 13).

- **Whether `full_auto`'s boundary question belongs on the same session-level footing as the egress gate after all.** ADR-0140 chose per-call, on the grounds that `fsMode` is a mid-session holder. If the holder is ever frozen for a session, the argument weakens and a remembered answer becomes defensible. Recorded so a later change is a decision rather than a drift.

The two items ADR-0141 left pending that this spec carried are **decided** (2026-10-15), and the third — where the whole-clear hook sits for permission changes that never persist — is closed by ADR-0141 ruling 3 and written up in §The three tiers and the presets.

- **No in-session `/no-sandbox` in this round** (ADR-0141 P6). The no-sandbox preset is entered only by `--no-sandbox` at launch; since it survives permission changes, `/permissions` and Shift+Tab adjust the permission axis under it without clearing it. The slash vocabulary and its pinned table (`src/tui/slash.ts:77`, `:130`; `tests/tui/slash.test.ts:1053`, `:1389-1393`) stay unchanged.
- **The posture is recorded on `SessionRecord` and shown in `/info`** (ADR-0141 P1). A new `posture` field on `SessionRecord` (`src/harness/trace/types.ts:243-255`, filled at `src/harness/loop-engine.ts:4506-4514`) and one added line in `/info` (`src/tui/app.tsx:6201-6210`). No `/status` command is added.
